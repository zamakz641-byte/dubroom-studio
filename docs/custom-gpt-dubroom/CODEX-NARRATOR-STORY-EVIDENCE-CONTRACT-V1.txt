DUBROOM NARRATOR STORY V1 - EVIDENCE CONTRACT

This mode is independent from Multi-Speaker V3.

INPUT
- Complete source story, not an arbitrary episodic fragment.
- Chinese ASR with timestamps.
- Chinese subtitle track with provenance.
- English subtitle track with provenance.
- Video-derived hard scene boundaries.
- Narration slots with safety margins and word budgets.
- Protected and duckable SFX intervals.

AUTHORITY
- Audio/ASR: audibility and spoken-event timing.
- Chinese subtitles: names, terminology and clean source wording.
- Uploader English subtitles: strong meaning aid, not automatic final prose.
- Auto-translated English: low-confidence clue only.
- Video analysis: absolute scene and narration boundaries.

OUTPUT
- One English narrator.
- One coherent full-story script.
- Dialogue converted mainly to indirect narration.
- Every block tied to one scene and one approved slot.
- Narrator subtitles reproduce the spoken narration.
- No block may cross its hard end.

NON-NEGOTIABLE
If a sentence does not fit, rewrite or remove lower-priority detail. Never extend the scene, freeze the image, retime the source or let narration continue after the visual beat has ended.
